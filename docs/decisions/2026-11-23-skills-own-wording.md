**The PV skills are deepened and extended in our own words; no text from external skill collections is used.** Ashu asked to compare the skills with a public collection of product-management skills and, if those were deeper, to update ours. They are deeper in craft (explained ideas, pitfalls, worked examples, question ladders) but their licence is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and says plainly that the skills may not be repackaged into a paid product without written permission, and share-alike would bind our own skills. Ashu chose to write our own (2026-11-23, "path 1"): the depth comes from general product practice and the public frameworks, the patterns (ideas, pitfalls, checks, examples, options, a read-back) are ours to use, and none of their wording, examples, names or structure is copied. The alternative, asking the author for a written commercial licence, was not chosen. Product Velocity's counsel should confirm the reading of the licence.
