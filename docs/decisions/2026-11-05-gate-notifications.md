**Gate notifications are an in-app inbox written by the database, with no email or Slack for now.** Why in-app and not email: sending to people needs an explicit confirmation (CLAUDE.md rule 3), and a notification fired by someone else's click is not that, so email waits for an opt-in with its own confirmation design. Why the database: triggers on `gate_reviews` notify from one place, so the single request, the bulk request, the dialogs and any later route behave the same, and the rule can be tested against the real tables. Recipients: a request notifies the people the requester named when they can decide that gate, otherwise everyone who can (Admins, the Approver permission, the gate's list); never the requester or a service account. A decision or a withdrawal notifies the requester; a decision or withdrawal also marks the approvers' notices read. A row holds the initiative code and name and the gate only, never a rationale, document text or commercial field. Notifications are derived data and not audited again (the request and decision are). Only the owner reads a row (RLS) and marks it read (`mark_notifications_read`); Claude and service accounts neither receive nor mark. Choices made without asking, change them if you disagree: the bell polls every minute (no realtime channel yet), the inbox keeps the latest 50, a requester who decides their own request is not told, and only three events notify (requested, decided, withdrawn): "hold due", "gate waiting" and "open conditions" stay on the Needs attention screen instead of becoming notices.
