**Jira follows the GitHub sync model, and what an initiative is in Jira is a per-customer mapping.** PV owns the initiative and its allowlisted fields; the tracker owns epics, tasks and all status. Sync is preview, conflicts, confirm (`docs/21`). The initiative maps to a Jira Epic (standard site), to a level above Epic (Premium or Enterprise hierarchy), or to a Jira Product Discovery idea (read-only until a spike shows writes work); epics and tasks shift down one level accordingly. Custom fields are discovered on first connection into a field catalog and mapped by choice. Sign in with Atlassian stays later.
