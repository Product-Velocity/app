**Discovery is a document, and only the chosen solution becomes an initiative.** Discovery documents are a new document kind `discovery`; key results (several, with guardrails) stay in the document, while the fields keep one Primary KR; the ranked candidate solutions stay in the document and are not registered as initiatives. Reason: Product Velocity has one Primary KR and ranks real initiatives globally; a discovery's ten options are analysis, not commitments. See `docs/24-CLAUDE-DISCOVERY-FLOW.md`.
