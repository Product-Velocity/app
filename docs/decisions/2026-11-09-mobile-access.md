**The phone is for deciding, looking up and capturing; planning and configuration stay on desktop** (`docs/29-MOBILE.md`). Answers to the open questions: (1) all three gates, A, B and C, can be decided on a phone; (2) phones use 15 to 16 px type and 44 px touch targets, an exception to the 13 px compact style; (3) **no push notifications** on mobile (the in-app inbox and email stay); (4) no PRD comments: Hold with a rationale is the way to send a PRD back; (5) a gate decision needs only the normal sign-in, no extra re-authentication, but passkey and biometric sign-in is wanted for the whole product (later slice); (6) a placeholder is an initiative that fails its stage check, there is no marker field; (7) tablets (700 px and wider) keep the desktop layout.
