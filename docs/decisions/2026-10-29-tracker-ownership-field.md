**Ownership of a linked item is a dictionary field: Tracker Ownership (N48, 170 fields).** It shows "Product Velocity" or "Tracker (read only)" next to Synced With in the Delivery view, so who owns a pulled-in or linked item is visible in the grid and not only in the item panel. It is derived from the link mode, not stored, and read-only in the grid (changed on the GitHub tab or at pull-in). The name says "Tracker" so it also fits Jira. The Source field stays free text.
