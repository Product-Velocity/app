**The Alignment tab is a table of every alignment session, designed like the other tabs, and who may read which session is decided by the database.** Issue #392. Ashu (2026-11-10): the header tab replaces the account-menu entry (`/alignment` stays); **the quarter column is the planned quarter, not the quarter the session closed in** (Ashu changed this the same day; first said the closing date); **members who are not admins see only sessions with results; drafts are admins only**. The points below were open; any can be reversed.

1. **Who reads what, in the database (migration `20261110115820_alignment_list_access.sql`).** Admins read every session. Everyone else reads only closed or archived sessions with `show_results` on. The initiatives of a session follow the session. `alignment_mailings` and `alignment_rank_applications` become admin-only (their existence or content would reveal a draft or a vote that a member may not see). Changed with `alter policy`, no drop. **This closes #400**: a viewer can no longer read `results_snapshot` of a session whose results are off.
2. **Admin-only figures come from a function**, `alignment_session_stats()`: invited, voted, a mailing waiting for a confirm, the latest application not undone and whether one was undone. Totals only, never per group, no name, email or ballot. Rows only for admins.
3. **Planned quarter** is derived, not stored: the **Planned Quarter of the session's initiatives** (the field `g2m_quarter`): the one most of them have, the earlier on a tie, with "+N" and the other quarters named when they differ; "No planned quarter" when none has one. The filter matches any of the quarters that occur. It is read as the initiatives are now (a later change of an initiative's planned quarter shows in the old session), which is the price of not storing it. **I read "planned quarter" as the initiatives' field, not a value typed on the session. If you want it set by hand on the session instead (for example "Q1 priorities" planning Q1), that is a new column and a small change to the session settings; say so.** Replaces the first decision (quarter of the closing date). Planning cycles (#121) may replace it.
4. **The member view is read-only.** A member's row opens the results page without the invited count, names, export, suggested order or applied history. A member sees the number who voted, not who was invited.
5. **"Needs you" (admins):** a draft with nobody invited; a mailing waiting for a confirm (draft or open session); an open session closing within 48 hours; a closed or archived session where people voted and the result is not applied (an undone one does not count as applied, and is not asked about again). Each chip is also a filter.
6. **New session** is a button on the tab with an initiative picker (up to 100). **The picker's filters are the ones people use most (Ashu, 2026-11-10): planned quarter, MoSCoW (Must, Should, Could, Won't, or not set) and stage chips: Discover, PRD (Define) and Ready for build**, plus project and a search. Within the stages the match is "any of them"; between the filters it is "all of them". "PRD (Define)" is Initiative Phase Define, where the PRD is written (`docs/28`); "Ready for build" is the computed field being Ready (set at Gate B). Each chip shows how many initiatives it matches. The grid's bulk action stays as the shortcut. Cycles (#121) will add a candidate pool.
7. **Layout.** A plain table with sort and filter chips (tens of rows a year; no AG Grid). On a phone the rows become cards. "Opened" is a sortable value in the logic but not a column, so the table fits a normal screen.
8. **Not built here:** a side panel for a session, saved views for this table, and a cycle-based New session.
