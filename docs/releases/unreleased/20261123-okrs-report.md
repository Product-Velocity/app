**New OKRs report.** The Reports tab has a sixth report, OKRs: the Objectives grouped by Business Line with their Focus Area, Owner, Business Value Type and number of initiatives; initiatives per Objective for each Planned Quarter, to see how the focus moves from quarter to quarter; and the Objectives of the current quarter split by Initiative Phase. It uses the shared filters, so click an Objective, Business Line, quarter or phase to filter. No migration.
