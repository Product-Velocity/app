**PV skills: `pv-premortem` and `pv-one-pager`.** Your AI assistant can now run guided skills on an initiative, started as `pv-<skill>` with an initiative code and your own words (add `--draft` to see the result first). Each skill reads the company profile and what Product Velocity already stores, asks one short message of skippable questions, and saves a document as an AI draft. `pv-premortem` turns an imagined failure into ranked risks with early signals; `pv-one-pager` asks for one decision on one page. `pv-help` lists the skills. In Claude and Gemini they are in the prompt menu; ChatGPT types the command. Help pages updated.
