**Cleaner initiative header, and documents you can read on a full page and copy.** The details panel header now reads top to bottom: navigation with "Open full page" and close, the code and project, the title, the rank, the status pills, the tracker lines, then the actions. Sync with Jira or GitHub is the one blue primary button, and Delete is a visible red button on the other side (it had lost its red inside the header). On the Spec tab, a document has "Open full page" (a wide reading page of its own, opening on the Preview, with Write and Preview) and two copy buttons: "Copy" puts the text on the clipboard with its headings and tables, ready to paste into an email, Google Docs, Word or Slack, and "Copy as Markdown" copies the source.
