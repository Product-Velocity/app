**Choose which way a Confluence document is kept up to date.** For each document linked to a Confluence page you can now choose: read in from Confluence and update from it, write from Product Velocity and update to it, or both ways. You pick it when you publish or import, and can change it later from the document’s menu (“Confluence sync…”), which also shows what each side has that the other lacks. Changes are found when you look (that window, or the Sync dialog, which now lists “Confluence pages to update” both to send and to receive), and every update in either direction is a preview you confirm. If both sides changed you choose which to keep, and the other text is not lost. The note “Managed in Product Velocity” on a page we wrote is not read back as text.
