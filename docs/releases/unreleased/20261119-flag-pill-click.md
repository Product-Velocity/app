**Click a flag to act on it.** In the Flags column of the Roadmap and of the Changes report, a flag that has a suggested next step (such as "No phase set") can be clicked and does what its button in the Actions column does: it makes the move, opens the gate request, or, once the gate is requested, opens the Decisions page. Flags with no next step stay plain labels.
