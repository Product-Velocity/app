**Velocity report: the time is the sum of the phases that have dates.** An initiative is no longer left out when it lacks Start Discovery or Start Roll-Out: every phase with a start and an end date counts, and the initiative's time is what they add up to (only Build dates give the build time, Discover and Build give both). Total, To Gate A, B and C add the dated phases up to that point, and Discover, Define, Build, Test and Experiment can each be shown on their own. A new chart, "Time in each phase", shows the average, median and the 10th and 90th percentile of every phase over the initiatives that have it, and the stages add up to the total.
