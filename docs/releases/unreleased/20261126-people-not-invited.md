**Invite the people you named, when you are ready.** Members has a new “Not invited yet” list (admins): everyone your team named as Product Manager, Engineering Manager, Designer, Analyst or Business Manager before they had an account, with how many initiatives name them. Add a person with a name and an optional email, edit or remove them, and press Invite… to send an invitation through the usual review step (nothing is sent until you confirm). When they join with that email everything that names them stays; if they join with another email, “Joined with another email” links them to that member.
