**Seven more skills: `pv-business-case`, `pv-pr-faq`, `pv-prototype`, `pv-opportunity-tree`, `pv-impact-map`, `pv-market` and `pv-canvas`.** On an initiative: `pv-business-case` compares the options (doing nothing is always the baseline) with costs, assumptions given as low, base and high and what would change the answer, and can fill Cost of not doing; `pv-pr-faq` writes the press release as if it had shipped with the customer and internal questions and the weakest claims; `pv-prototype` writes a test card whose pass line is set before testing; `pv-opportunity-tree` goes from one outcome to customer opportunities, the one to pursue and three solution ideas with their riskiest assumption. For the whole workspace: `pv-impact-map` maps one goal to the behaviour changes that serve it and flags roadmap work that serves none, `pv-market` sizes a market as ranges with sources, and `pv-canvas` fills a Lean Canvas and/or a value proposition canvas with each box marked tested or untested. They never invent figures, quotes or results, and never touch Stack Rank, Value or Confidence. Six new document kinds (migration `20261121215823_document_more_skill_kinds.sql`). Help has a table of every skill, a "which skill when" table along the product life cycle and tips for getting the most out of them (AI co-workers and AI use-cases pages).
