**Passkey autofill on the sign-in page.** Click the email field and, if you have a passkey for Product Velocity on this device or in your password manager (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, 1Password and others), the browser lists it there; choose it and you are signed in. Nothing appears when there is no passkey, and it works on a device that has never used the site. The "Sign in with a passkey" button stays on devices where you added or used one.
