**Cards redesigned.** Each card now has four parts: rank, initiative ID and the GitHub / Jira IDs (both clickable) with health; the name; up to five chosen fields; and the owner. MoSCoW shows as a pill, the size stays a circle, and dates, percentages and teams are drawn by type. The new "Card fields: N of 5" picker shows how many slots are used, previews a real card and sets the order. A ⋯ menu replaces "Move…". The board also shows a health edge on each card, days in stage, sizes per column, a health bar per lane and a drop zone while dragging.
