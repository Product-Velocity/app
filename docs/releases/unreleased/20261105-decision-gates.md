**Decision gates and one lifecycle (issue #323).** Initiatives now move Discover, Define, Build, Test, Experiment, Roll-out and Completed (Beta-test is now Test; Sunset is gone; Completed follows Initiative Status). Three gates, A (Discover to Define), B (Define to Build, where the PRD is approved and the initiative becomes Ready for build) and C (Experiment to Roll-out), are requested by a Creator and decided by anyone with approval rights for that gate (Admins by default, set per gate in Settings, Workspace; you may approve your own request; Claude never can). New **Decisions** screen with Waiting for approval, Decided, Requested by me and Can decide, a gate pack in the peek panel and keyboard decisions; Request gate, Decide and Move to next phase in the bulk bar and item panel; the Initiative Phase cell is read-only. Fast lane is recorded by the approver on the decision, with no checks. The Spec Status field is removed (the PRD status is the approval). Nothing is synced from Discover; an initiative in Define is labelled pv:define for engineering review; syncing stops 14 days after Completed or Cancelled (an admin or approver can resume), and Completed initiatives are hidden from the grid unless you include them. New read-only Claude tool get_gate_pack. Migrations `phase_values_test_completed`, `decision_gates` and `tracker_freeze`; the Help page has a new Decision gates section.
