**Deeper skills, and five new ones.** `pv-premortem`, `pv-metrics` and `pv-one-pager` now carry the craft behind the method: the key ideas, when not to use the skill, the mistakes that spoil it, a check before delivery, a strong and a weak example and a worked example. Questions offer lettered options with when each fits, and every skill opens its one message by reading back what it found, what it is assuming, what is missing and what conflicts, so you correct it in a word. New: `pv-gate` writes the decision brief for the person who approves a gate (the evidence, the gaps, Go, Go with conditions, Hold and Stop for this initiative, and a proposed recommendation; it never requests or decides the gate), `pv-prioritise` chooses an approach that fits your stage and data and proposes a ranking with its inputs, weak points and alignment check (advisory; it never sets Stack Rank), `pv-hypothesis` turns a belief into a testable IF / THEN / RESULTING IN and fills the Hypothesis field after a preview, `pv-outcome` prepares the 30, 60 and 90 day outcome review (figures only as you state them), and `pv-which` recommends the right skill for your situation. Four new document kinds (migration `20261123084439_document_wave1_skill_kinds.sql`). Help lists the new skills. **A quarter always means the Planned Quarter field:** ask for "the Q4-26 roadmap with statuses" (or `pv-weekly Q4-26`) and your assistant fetches the initiatives by Planned Quarter first, whatever the spelling (Q4-26, Q4 2026, Q4'26), never by dates, and lists initiatives without a Planned Quarter apart. Overviews now show both the initiative status and the build status.
