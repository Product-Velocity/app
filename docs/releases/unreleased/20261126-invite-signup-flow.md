**Smoother invitation sign-up.** After you create your account from an invitation you now get a "Check your email" screen that explains the next step, lets you resend the link, and moves on by itself when you confirm in the same browser. The emailed link opens a Continue page, so company mail scanners can no longer use the one-time link up before you do, and pressing Continue signs you in and accepts the invitation in one step (two-factor still comes first). If a link was already used, you land on your invitation with the sign-in form instead of a bare sign-in page. Security is unchanged.
