**A branded loader for loading and sync.** Wherever Product Velocity is loading, saving or syncing, you now see the logo's pills light up one by one next to a short word ("Loading the roadmap…", "Saving…", "Checking GitHub…"). It also appears on buttons while they work and on the refresh button, and the browser tab says "Syncing…" during a sync. It only shows after a short wait, so quick loads do not flicker, and with reduced motion the pills stay still.
