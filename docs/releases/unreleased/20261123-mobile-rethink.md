**New phone experience.** The phone no longer sends you to a computer. The bottom bar is now Initiatives, Decide, Add, Tracker and Insights. Initiatives opens on the 15 most recently changed initiatives and says what changed and when; search still finds any initiative. The new Tracker tab shows the latest 10 changes from GitHub and Jira, with a search for a tracker item. The roadmap grid and the Gantt open the Initiatives list on a phone, and the More screen lists only what works there.
