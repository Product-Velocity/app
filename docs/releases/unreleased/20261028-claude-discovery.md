**Claude can write your discovery documents (issue #244).** A discovery is the step before the PRD: objective and key results, problem, evidence, risks, ranked potential solutions, next steps and a test plan. Ask Claude to "write a discovery for ..." (or use the `write_discovery` prompt) and it reads your brief and sources, asks once for what is missing (placeholders like "X" stay "to define"), and creates one initiative in the Discover phase that holds the discovery as a new kind of document, **Discovery**. The document stays in Product Velocity and never goes to GitHub; only the solution you choose becomes an initiative, and the other solutions stay in the document. Claude never sets stack rank, impact, confidence or decisions. The PRD template now has an **Objective and key results** block, a test plan, and optional Evidence and Competitive landscape sections, and Claude reads an existing discovery before writing the PRD.
