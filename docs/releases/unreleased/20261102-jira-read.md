**Read Jira (issue #67, third part).** On Settings, Integrations, Jira an admin can press "Read Jira now" on a linked project. The open issues of that Jira project are read into a list (key, title, type, status, parent) and the page shows when it was read, how many issues are new or changed, and how many are not pulled in yet. Nothing is created in the roadmap and nothing is written to Jira; choosing what to pull in comes next.
