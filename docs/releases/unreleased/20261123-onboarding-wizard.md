**A clearer start for new workspaces.** Setting up now has a progress bar (Workspace, About you), regional settings tucked away until you need them, and a short optional About you step (name, role, what to fix first). On the roadmap, admins see a dismissable "Get started" card: add your first initiative, invite your team, connect GitHub or Jira, with links to connect Claude and to Help. The product owner gets an email when someone finishes setting up a workspace.

People who join by invitation now see what happens next before they sign in, and a short welcome after they accept: their role, how to start, and an optional name and role.

Setting up a new workspace now also walks the admin through inviting their team (every invitation is reviewed before it is sent) and connecting GitHub, Jira or Claude, each optional.

Two personal plain-text emails from Ashu (a welcome after setup, and one check-in two days later for a workspace that is still empty) are ready and stay off until they are switched on.
