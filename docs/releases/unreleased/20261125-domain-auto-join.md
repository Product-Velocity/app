**Colleagues at your domain join automatically as Viewers.** Someone who signs up with a confirmed email at one of your authorized domains (or verifies such a linked address) now gets in as a Viewer straight away, and your admins get an email to set other rights in Members. A new Workspace settings switch, on by default, turns it off to keep the approval step. Migration `20261125104319_domain_auto_join.sql`.
