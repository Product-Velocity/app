**Flagged fields at the top of the initiative panel.** When you open an initiative (from its ID, or from a flag's action that needs you to type something), a new "Needs your input" section sits under the Phase and gate line. It lists each flag the initiative carries with the reason, and the fields the flag is about, ready to set there: a dropdown or an open field, the same editors as the Overview. For example: No Business Owner (the owner), No key result (the metric, unit, direction, start and target), Tracker done, initiative open (the status), No health set and Delivery risk (health and the G2M date). Flags with nothing to type (a phase to move, a gate to request) say so and point to the buttons above.
