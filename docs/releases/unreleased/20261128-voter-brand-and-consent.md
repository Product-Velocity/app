**Voters see your company, and nothing asks them to accept anything by default.** When your brand is confirmed and switched on for voting, the voting screens and the invitation, reminder and new-link emails carry your logo, name and colours (always checked for readability) and no Product Velocity branding; each session can opt out. A vote no longer asks voters to accept wording first: a vote inside your company runs under your own policies, and voters just press Start voting. Turn on "Ask voters to accept before they vote" for a session that includes people from outside the company. With the step off, an admin can tick at setup that the company's policy covers the vote, which lets Creators open who voted what after the close (admins always can, with a reason). Show names now also lists a voter's MoSCoW choice. Existing sessions keep the step they ran with. Migration `20261128133230_alignment_brand_and_consent`.
