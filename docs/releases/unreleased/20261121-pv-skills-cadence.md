**Three skills for the rhythm of the product: `pv-weekly`, `pv-release-notes` and `pv-quarter`, a new `get_report` tool, and a skills table in Help.** `pv-weekly` and `pv-release-notes` are reports: they produce the content in the format you ask for (email, slides, a document, a Slack message or just the chat; add `--format=email`) and save nothing unless you ask for a copy; if a presentation, document, Gmail or Slack tool is connected they can create a file or a draft, never send. `pv-weekly` reads the same weekly digest and roadmap health the app shows, through the new read-only `get_report` tool, so its numbers match the Leadership page. `pv-release-notes` writes customer notes from customer-safe fields plus an internal version for support. `pv-quarter` drafts objectives and key results marked committed or aspirational, a capacity split from the numbers you give, the bets and exclusions, and how each result will be graded, and saves it as a workspace document (Documents in the account menu). Results belong to teams, never to people, and nothing changes Stack Rank, Value or Confidence. Help now has a table of every skill, what it works on and what you get.
