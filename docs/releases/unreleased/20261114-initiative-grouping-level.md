**Issues now group under their initiative.** "Initiative, with its issues" is a choice in Group by, as the main level or the "Then by" level, instead of a separate checkbox. Each initiative becomes a collapsible header (its issue count and status in words, a click on the name opens it) with its linked Jira or GitHub epics, stories and tasks nested beneath it, for example Build Status › Initiative, with its issues. The "Show issues" checkbox from 1.144.0 is gone.
