**Show issues is a third level under each initiative, and the Group by panel is staged.** The "Show issues" checkbox is back in Group by: when on, every initiative keeps its row and gets an "Issues of R-01" header beneath it (issue count and status in words) with its Jira or GitHub epics, stories and tasks nested under it; each initiative's issues collapse and expand. It works under any grouping. The Group by panel now works like Columns: choose both levels and the issues option, then Apply (Cancel discards, Clear empties it); the panel stays open until then.
