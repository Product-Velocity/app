**The passkey button on the sign-in page appears only where you have a passkey.** Pressing it without a passkey on that device led to a QR-code sheet. Now the button shows on a device after you add a passkey there (Security) or sign in with one, and disappears if the passkey is not found or you remove your last one. Add a passkey on each device you sign in from.
