**Initiatives on your phone (M2 of the mobile work).** On a phone the Cards screen becomes a searchable list: a search box that stays on top, quick-filter chips (Mine, New this week, Needs attention, Waiting for a decision, and one per phase), Sort by rank or newest, and groups by phase, project or quarter that you can fold. Tap a card to open the initiative, with its gate strip and tabs laid out for a small screen. Computers and tablets are unchanged.
