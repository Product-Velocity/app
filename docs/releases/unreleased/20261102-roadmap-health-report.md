**Reports now has a list of reports, and a new Roadmap Health report.** Next to the Reports title you pick Overview (opens first, unchanged) or Roadmap Health; filters and click-to-filter pills carry over. Roadmap Health shows active initiatives, how many are Blocked, At Risk or Delayed, active initiatives by team and phase and by team and health, the median days spent in the current phase, the initiatives that have waited longest, and how long completed initiatives took. Initiatives without a usable date are counted and shown, not hidden.
