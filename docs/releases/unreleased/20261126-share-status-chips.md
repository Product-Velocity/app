**The Spec tab now says what happened, not only what is switched on.** The chip on a document, file or link used to say “Shared with Jira” even when nothing had ever been sent (for example a read-only Jira project). It now says what will happen or what did, and a line under the entry gives the date and time: “In the Jira description · Sent 26 Nov 2026, 14:05”, “Goes in at next send”, “Goes in with the issue”, “Not sent: read-only”, or “Not in the Jira issue”. Documents that live in Confluence say so too, with the page, its version and when it was last written or read (“Copied from Confluence…” and “Kept in sync with the page…”), instead of “Only in Product Velocity”. A link says “Goes in the Jira description” with the time of the last send. GitHub shows the time of its last write as well; Jira now records it.
