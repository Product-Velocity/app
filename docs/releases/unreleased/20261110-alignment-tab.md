**The Alignment tab (issue #392).** Alignment is now a tab in the header, next to Cards and Leadership, and no longer an entry in the account menu. It lists every alignment session, past and present, with method, status, created and closing dates, the **planned quarter** (the Planned Quarter most of its initiatives have), initiatives, how many groups disagreed, and, for admins, people invited, voted, participation, whether the result was applied to the Stack Rank and who created it. Admins also get a strip of totals for the sessions shown and "needs you" chips (a draft with nobody invited, a mailing waiting for your confirm, a session closing within 48 hours, a closed one not applied yet) that filter the table. Search finds a session by its name or by an initiative in it; filter by status, method, planned quarter, applied or not and who created it; sort any column; archived sessions are behind a switch. **New session** opens a dialog to name it, choose the method and pick the initiatives, finding them by planned quarter, MoSCoW (for example Must) and the stages Discover, PRD (Define) and Ready for build, and the roadmap's "Start alignment session…" still works. On a phone the rows become cards. Who sees what is now decided by the database: admins see every session, everyone else only closed sessions that allow results, in a read-only results view without the invited count, names or export. This also fixes the gap where a member could read the stored results of a session whose results were switched off (#400). Migration `20261110115820_alignment_list_access.sql` (changes four read rules in place and adds an admin-only stats function; nothing dropped).
