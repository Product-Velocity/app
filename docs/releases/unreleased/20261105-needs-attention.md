**Needs attention screen (issue #337).** A new tab lists the initiatives that need a person to look, worst first: delivery risk, build done in the tracker while the phase is still Build, tracker work before the gate, a cancelled initiative that is still being built, a Hold whose review date has come, a gate request waiting more than a week, open gate conditions, and initiatives stalled in a phase. Each entry says why in words and offers the next step in one click (Move to Test, Request gate, open the initiative or Decisions); nothing changes until you press it. J and K move through the list, ⌘K has "Go to Needs attention", and the Help page explains each reason.
