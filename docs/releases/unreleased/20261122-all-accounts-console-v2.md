**All accounts console v2 (platform owner).** The page is now the same AG Grid and action bar as the Roadmap (Filter with quick filters and rules, Columns, Group by, fold all, Search, filter chips) plus CSV export. New columns: created date, company URL, line items, pending invites, access requests, last login, which AI agents are connected (with their connections and calls), GitHub and Jira state, status and country. Each row has Enter and Manage; the Manage drawer shows the account's details, lets you enter it, pause or resume access, set the plan, restrict platform access to read-only, delete it with the typed name, and see its people: invite someone (link or email), renew or cancel an invitation, and pause, resume or revoke a person (never roles or Approver). A paused account gives its members nothing and shows them a plain "This account is paused" screen; platform admins can still enter. Migration `20261122153043_platform_accounts_console.sql`.
