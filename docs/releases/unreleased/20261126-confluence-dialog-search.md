**Easier to find a space and a page when publishing to Confluence.** The space is one search box (type part of the name, Enter to pick; the last space you used comes first). The page tree has its own search that looks through the folders and pages of the space and opens the way to a match, a line under it shows where the page will go, and the list is taller with long titles cut short. The same space search is used when importing a page.
