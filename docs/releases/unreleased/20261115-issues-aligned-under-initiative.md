**Show issues is easier to read.** The issues now line up with the grid's columns, so they read as children of the initiative above: the key under Initiative ID, the type and title under Initiative Name behind a branch line, then the status and the assignee. They are in smaller, muted type on a light tint, and a firmer line above each initiative makes it one block with its issues. The separate "Issues of R-26" row is gone: a chip on the initiative's name, "▾ 7 issues · 2 To Do · 5 Done", folds and unfolds that initiative's issues. The command palette can also collapse or expand all issues at once.
