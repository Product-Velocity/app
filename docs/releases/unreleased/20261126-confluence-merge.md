**Merge a document and its Confluence page when both changed.** For a document kept both ways with its page, if both sides changed since they were last in step, the Confluence sync window now offers “Merge the two texts…”. Changes in different places come through on their own; where both sides changed the same block you see the two side by side and pick Product Velocity’s, Confluence’s or both. One Confirm saves the merged text as a new version of the document and writes it to the page, so both end identical; the text each side had stays as a previous version and in the page history. Nothing is written before Confirm, and Claude cannot merge.
