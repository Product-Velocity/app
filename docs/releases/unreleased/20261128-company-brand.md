**Your company's brand lives in the company profile.** A new Brand card on Company profile holds your logo, main and accent colour and fonts. Read your website, or a shared PowerPoint or Word file, and it proposes the name, colours, fonts and logo; you choose what is right, save a draft and an admin confirms it. Whatever colours you pick are checked for readability, and the preview shows what the screens will really use and what was adjusted. A confirmed brand is what the voting screens, the voter emails and, where you switch it on, the whole interface will use in the next steps; Claude can propose a brand but never confirm it.
