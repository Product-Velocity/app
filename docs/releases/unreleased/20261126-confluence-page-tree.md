**Choose the Confluence page, not paste it.** Publish to Confluence now shows the pages of the space as a tree (open a page to see its sub pages) so you pick the parent. You can also publish onto an existing page: the preview names the page and says its content is replaced (Confluence keeps the old version in the page history), and only Confirm writes. The page title starts as “Document type - R-ID - Initiative name” and can be changed.
