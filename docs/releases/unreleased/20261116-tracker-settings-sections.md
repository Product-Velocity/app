**GitHub and Jira settings are easier to work with.** Each now has five sections in setup order (Connection, Projects, Field mapping, Inbox, Sync & logs), a status header with the counts that matter and a next step, and uses the full page width. Field mapping is one table per project: every Product Velocity field, where it goes and its setting, with filters (it opens on what Sync moves), search, an unsaved-changes bar and "Copy to other projects". Projects and repositories get search and paging. No migration.
