**Group by in two levels, a categorised picker, and issues under initiatives.** Group by now takes a second level ("Then by") with an indented sub-header and its own count and health words; the picker lists fields under the same categories as the Columns picker, with a Common section on top and the two levels shown as slots. A new option, "Show issues under each initiative", lists the linked Jira or GitHub epics, stories and tasks beneath each initiative in the grid. The Filter and Columns buttons now say their state like Group by does ("Filter: 1 active", "Columns: 14 of 168 shown"). Saved views keep the second level and the issues option (migration `20261114224144_group_then_by_and_show_issues`).
