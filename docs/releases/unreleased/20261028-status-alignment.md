**Status alignment (issue #247).** Three statuses now have one clear job each. **Build Status** is what the tracker says and has four fixed values: Not started, In progress, Done, Cancelled (a closed GitHub issue gives Done, closed as not planned gives Cancelled). **Initiative Status** is what only a person knows: Active, On hold or Cancelled (Completed now comes from Initiative Phase). **Planning Status** is available now and starts the list: Backlog, Not started, then Candidate, Included, Committed, Deferred, Dropped. Existing initiatives are converted by the migration (Backlog and Not Started move to Planning Status, In Progress becomes Active, Paused becomes On hold, a Completed initiative with no phase gets the phase Completed) and every change is in the audit log. In the default views Planning Status takes the place Initiative Status had in Delivery and History, and Initiative Status the place of Planning Status in Planning and Cycle review. Delivery Status is switched off for now (not in the Delivery view or the panel header; the field and the board read are kept). The "In progress" quick filter is now "Active".
