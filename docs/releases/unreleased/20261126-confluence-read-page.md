**Read a Confluence page into a document.** On the Spec tab, "Import from Confluence…" lets you browse a space and its sub pages (or paste an address), choose what kind of document it is, and see a preview with what could not be carried over (images, some macros, merged cells). Confirm copies the text into a new document that stays out of the tracker until you share it. With "Keep in sync" you can check the page later for a newer version and update the document after a preview. The page itself is only read, never changed. Claude can read a page that is linked on an initiative. Reconnect Confluence under Settings, Integrations, Confluence if the permission to read pages is missing.
