**Fix: Confluence pages inside folders are now found.** In a space that keeps its pages in folders, the page tree for importing and publishing showed only the top-level page. It now lists the folders too, opens them to their pages and sub folders, and shows the pages as before when folders cannot be read. Folders can be opened but not chosen. To see folders, reconnect Confluence once (Settings, Integrations, Confluence) after an admin adds the two new permissions to the Atlassian app.
