**Install the app, share into it, and sign in with a passkey (M6 of the mobile work).** Product Velocity can be added to a phone's home screen and opens on For you. On Android, sharing text or a link from another app offers Product Velocity and opens the Add screen with the name and the "what and why" line filled in, ready to check and create. The sign-in page has a "Sign in with a passkey" button (face, fingerprint, PIN or security key) and Security lists your passkeys with Add, Rename and Remove. Passkeys work once an admin of the platform has switched them on in Supabase; until then the screens say so.
