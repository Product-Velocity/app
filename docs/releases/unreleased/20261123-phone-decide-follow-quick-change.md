**Decide, follow and quick changes on the phone.** The Decide tab now has Gates and Alignment results: each session with how many voted and its top initiatives. You can follow an initiative from its briefing; followed initiatives come first in Recently changed and have their own Following filter. Creators and admins can change Health or Status from the briefing with a short reason (needed for At Risk, Delayed, Blocked, On hold and Cancelled), which shows under the change; Put it back undoes it. Migration `quick_change_note`. The gate decision sheet's options are laid out properly on a phone.
