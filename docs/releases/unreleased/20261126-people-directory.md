**Name people before they are members.** Product Manager, Engineering Manager, Designer, Analyst and Business Manager can now name someone who has no account yet: pick “Add” in the picker (or “Not on the list? Add someone” in the item panel) and type a name, and an email address if you have one. They are marked “not invited”, appear in the pickers, filters, reports and exports by name, and nothing is sent to them. When they join with that email, everything that names them stays. Claude can name people on the list too. A member who leaves now stays on the initiatives as a name instead of being cleared. Migration `20261126093428_people_directory`.
