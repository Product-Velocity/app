**Five more skills.** `pv-split` cuts a large initiative into thin, usable slices in order, with what is deferred and why (nothing is created unless you agree after a preview). `pv-assumptions` lists what must be true for an initiative to work, ranks the riskiest and proposes the cheapest test for each. `pv-sunset` plans the retirement of a feature or product: who is affected, what depends on it, the path for customers, the timeline, the messages and the data questions (it never moves the phase or tells a customer). `pv-stakeholders` maps who must be aligned, what each group cares about and what to bring to each (roles only; alignment results stay anonymous aggregates). `pv-readiness` checks launch readiness area by area with a source, an owner and a date for each row, and offers to fill the enablement notes after a preview; it never says an initiative is ready and never moves a gate. Five new document kinds (migration `20261123091554_document_wave2_skill_kinds.sql`). Help lists the new skills.
