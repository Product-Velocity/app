**MoSCoW is a new way to vote in an alignment session.** Each person chooses Must, Should, Could or Won't for every initiative (there is no "no opinion"), on their phone like the other methods. The results page shows a suggested MoSCoW per initiative with the share of each choice; a tie goes to the more cautious choice and is marked contested, as is an initiative that got both Must and Won't. The vote only suggests: setting the MoSCoW field comes next. Migration `20261128115001_alignment_moscow`.
