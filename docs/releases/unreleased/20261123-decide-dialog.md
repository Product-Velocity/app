**A clearer Decide dialog, and batch decisions are easier to find.** The dialog is now a compact column instead of a full-width sheet: the requests with their gate and checklist result, four outcome cards (Go, Go with conditions, Hold, Stop), a plain sentence on what the chosen outcome will do, and the rationale. Ctrl or ⌘ + Enter saves. Choosing several requests says plainly that the outcome and rationale are saved on each one, and the Decisions list offers "select all you can decide". Fixes radio buttons that stretched across the dialog on iPad and desktop.
