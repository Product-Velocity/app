**Alignment tab is now a grid.** The list of alignment sessions uses the same grid and action bar as the Roadmap: Filter, Columns, Group by (status, method, planned quarter, month created, applied, what needs you) and Search, with the totals and "needs you" chips kept on top. Each session shows what needs you in words and an Actions column: add people, review the mailing, apply results, see results, and open, close or archive the session after a confirm. The name, the counts and the row itself open the session, its people or its results. Your columns and grouping are remembered in the browser, and Export CSV downloads the sessions shown.
