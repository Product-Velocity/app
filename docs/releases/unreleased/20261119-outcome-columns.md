**Outcome review columns in the Roadmap.** Five new read-only columns, in the Outcomes view and in the Columns picker: Next Outcome Review, Outcome Reviews Recorded (for example 1 of 3), Latest Realised KR Value, Latest Adoption % and KR Progress (how far the latest value is from the key result's start to its target). They are worked out from the 30, 60 and 90 day reviews, can be filtered, sorted and exported, and are empty for an initiative that has not reached Roll-out.
