The problem

Product teams move fast. Their tools do not.

Roadmap and delivery drift apart
The roadmap lives in slides and spreadsheets. Delivery lives in GitHub. Nobody sees the same picture.
Priorities follow the loudest voice
Stakeholders and customers are consulted late, if at all. Teams are surprised by what ships and when.
The story stops at launch
Few teams measure adoption and outcomes, so the next plan repeats the same guesses.
And now AI agents join the team. The tools were not built for that.
