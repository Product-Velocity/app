How an initiative moves

One roadmap for humans and AI.

Every initiative moves through the same clear phases. People decide at three gates. AI co-workers prepare the work. It does not end at shipped.

Decide with the facts
Discovery, evidence, ranking and capacity in one place. Leadership, teams and customers see the same priorities before anything is built.
Deliver in the tools you use
Work flows to GitHub, Jira and Bitbucket and progress flows back. Product Velocity works with your delivery tools, not instead of them.
Learn after launch
Experiment against a threshold you set beforehand, track go-to-market and adoption, and review outcomes at 30, 60 and 90 days. What you learn feeds the next plan.
People decide. AI co-workers prepare.
At every gate, AI co-workers can draft the documents and gather the evidence. A person with approval rights chooses Go, Go with conditions, Hold or Stop, and the reason is kept.
