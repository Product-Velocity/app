Each gate decision
Go
The initiative moves to the next phase.
Go with conditions
It moves, and every condition becomes a tracked task with an owner.
Hold
It stays where it is, with a review date.
Stop
It is cancelled; closing the tracker issue is offered, never done silently.
The decision and the reason are written once and never edited. People with approval rights for that gate decide; leadership can hold that right. AI co-workers prepare the gate pack and never decide. An approver can use the fast lane to skip phases, with a comment on the record.
