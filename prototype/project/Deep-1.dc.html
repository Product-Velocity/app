Decision gates keep speed and control

◆Gate 1
Worth exploring
Discover to Define
•Evidence of the problem
•Hypothesis written
•Why is aligned
Decided by Product lead
◆Gate 2
Commit to build
Define to Build
•Spec and criteria approved
•What and How aligned
•Fits team capacity
•Teams confirm When
Decided by An Approver
◆Gate 3
Ready to launch
Build to Roll-out
•Acceptance criteria met
•Go-to-market materials ready
•Success metrics measurable
Decided by Approver and go-to-market lead
◆Gate 4
Outcome review
At +30, +60 and +90 days
•Adoption vs target
•Key result vs target
•Customer feedback
Decided by Product lead and leadership
Every gate ends in a logged decision: Go, Go with conditions, Hold or Stop, with who, when and why. Conditions become tracked tasks.
Built for speed: a gate passes automatically when its criteria are met and nobody objects. Small items use a fast lane with only gate 2.
